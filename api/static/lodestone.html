Lodestone

Lodestone is an idea generator, not a detector. It scans every skill for conditions and item pairs that Path of Building scores unusually high and lists them for a person to check. Most flags are ordinary; some are Path of Building errors. It can't see exploits Path of Building doesn't calculate (copies, charge loops, bugs). Expect it to miss most of what breaks the game.

Numbers are from Path of Building. Anything marked unconfirmed is an idea, not a result: nobody has checked it in the game.
